Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 2026

These Child Safety Standards apply to the Ethos mobile application (the "App"), published on Google Play by Ethos Labs Pty Ltd (the "Developer", "we", "us"). They describe how Ethos prevents, detects, and responds to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on our platform, in accordance with the Google Play Child Safety Standards Policy and applicable law.

1. Zero-Tolerance Prohibition of CSAE and CSAM

Ethos and Ethos Labs Pty Ltd maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation. The following are expressly prohibited on Ethos and will result in immediate, permanent removal and reporting to authorities:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including AI-generated, computer-generated, animated, or otherwise synthetic depictions
  • Sexually explicit content depicting, describing, or involving any person under the age of 18
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation, sextortion, or any sexual communication directed at a minor
  • Trafficking, recruitment, or facilitation of child sexual exploitation
  • Sharing personal information of minors for the purpose of sexual contact
  • Use of the App by, or impersonation of, anyone under the age of 18

Ethos is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. Any account suspected to belong to a minor will be suspended and terminated.

2. Age Assurance and Verification

Ethos uses an age-attested onboarding process: users must declare their date of birth and confirm they are 18 or older before creating a profile. Profile photos are reviewed for indicators that a user may be a minor, and accounts where this is suspected are immediately suspended pending further review or removed entirely.

3. Content Moderation and Detection

All user-uploaded photos on Ethos are scanned by automated AI moderation (Sightengine) before becoming visible to other users. Images flagged for nudity, suggestive depictions of minors, or any content indicative of CSAM are automatically rejected, the uploading account is suspended, and the content is referred to our Trust & Safety team for human review and lawful reporting.

4. In-App Reporting Mechanism

Every Ethos profile and conversation includes an in-app Report action. Reporting categories specifically include "inappropriate content involving a minor", "suspected minor on the app", and "fake profile". Reports relating to child safety are escalated immediately and triaged by our Trust & Safety team within 24 hours.

You can also report suspected CSAE or CSAM directly to Ethos Labs Pty Ltd by emailing support@ethosapp.co with the subject line "Child Safety Report".

5. Reporting to Authorities

Ethos Labs Pty Ltd complies with all applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions where the App operates. We report any apparent or confirmed CSAM and credible CSAE concerns to the relevant authorities, including:

  • The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline in the United States
  • The Australian Centre to Counter Child Exploitation (ACCCE) and the eSafety Commissioner in Australia
  • The Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) and applicable law enforcement in the United Kingdom
  • Equivalent national authorities and law enforcement in users' home jurisdictions, as applicable

We preserve evidence of reported content in accordance with legal requirements and cooperate with valid law enforcement requests and investigations.

6. Enforcement and Account Action

Accounts found to violate these standards are permanently banned from Ethos. The associated user is prohibited from creating new accounts, and we retain device, IP, and identifier data to enforce this ban and to support investigations.

7. Training and Governance

Trust & Safety personnel at Ethos Labs Pty Ltd receive guidance on identifying CSAE and CSAM, lawful handling of such material, and the correct escalation paths to NCMEC and other authorities. These standards are reviewed by company leadership at least annually.
