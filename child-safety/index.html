Child Safety Standards

Last updated: April 2026

Ethos is committed to creating a safe environment for our community. This document outlines our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Ethos prohibits any content, conduct, or behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers minors. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Sexually explicit content depicting or involving minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexual communication with minors
  • Use of the platform by anyone under the age of 18

Ethos is intended for users 18 years of age and older. Any account suspected to belong to a minor will be terminated.

2. Age Verification

Ethos uses an age-attested onboarding process: users must declare their date of birth and confirm they are 18 or older before creating a profile. Profile photos are reviewed to detect indicators that a user may be a minor, and accounts where this is suspected are immediately suspended pending further review.

3. Content Moderation

All user-uploaded photos are scanned by automated AI moderation (Sightengine) before becoming visible to other users. Photos containing nudity, suggestive depictions of minors, or content otherwise indicative of CSAM are automatically rejected and the uploading account is flagged for human review.

4. Reporting Mechanisms

Every Ethos profile has an in-app Report action with categories including "inappropriate content involving a minor" and "fake profile". Reports are reviewed by our trust and safety team within 24 hours.

If you believe child safety material is on the platform, you can also email us directly at james@ethosapp.co.

5. Reporting to Authorities

Ethos complies with all applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions where we operate. We report any confirmed CSAM to:

  • The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United States
  • The eSafety Commissioner in Australia
  • Equivalent national authorities in users' home jurisdictions, as applicable

We retain evidence of reported content as required by law and cooperate with law enforcement investigations.

6. Account Action

Accounts found to violate these standards are permanently banned and the user is prohibited from creating new accounts. We retain device, IP, and identifier data to enforce this ban.

8. Updates

These standards are reviewed and updated periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
